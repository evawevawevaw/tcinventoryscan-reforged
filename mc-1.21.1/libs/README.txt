Put the Thaumcraft 4: Reforged jar here, named exactly as reforged_jar in gradle.properties
(default: thaumcraft-4-reforged-1.1-1.21.1.jar).
Take it from the mc/1.21.1 release of https://github.com/notlikethiss/thaumcraft-4-reforged (tag 1.21.1) or build it there with: ./gradlew build
The jar is in build/libs. Do not redistribute it: Reforged is personal-use only.
